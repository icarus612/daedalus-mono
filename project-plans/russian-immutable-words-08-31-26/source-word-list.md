This document contains the comprehensive lists of Russian invariable words (words that do not change endings for case, gender, tense, or number) as well as a guide on configuring Anki to generate high-quality, native-sounding Russian audio.

## Part 2: Generating Russian Audio with Anki Plugins

Correct pronunciation and stress are critical in Russian. Instead of relying on poor-quality default text-to-speech, you can use Anki plugins to generate highly accurate, neural-AI voices. The two best plugins are **AwesomeTTS** and **HyperTTS**. (Note: HyperTTS is actually the modern successor to AwesomeTTS, built by the same developer).

### How to Install Add-ons in Anki
1. Open Anki on your desktop.
2. Go to **Tools** > **Add-ons**.
3. Click **Get Add-ons**.
4. You will be prompted for a code. (See codes below).
5. Restart Anki after installation.

---

### Option 1: AwesomeTTS
AwesomeTTS is the classic, long-standing plugin for generating audio in Anki. 
**Add-on Code:** `1436550454`

#### Pros:
* Completely free for basic built-in OS voices and some free APIs.
* Very straightforward interface.
* Generates audio on the fly directly on the card while reviewing (if you don't want to store audio files).

#### Cons:
* Older interface, lacking modern batch-processing features.
* Access to premium AI voices (like Azure or Google Wavenet) requires setting up API keys manually or paying for an AwesomeTTS Plus subscription.

#### How to use it:
1. Open the Anki Browser (click **Browse**).
2. Select the cards you want to add audio to.
3. In the top menu, click **AwesomeTTS** -> **Add Audio to Selected**.
4. Choose your Source (e.g., Google Translate, Yandex, or a local OS voice). **Tip for Russian:** Yandex is often superior to Google for Russian text-to-speech.
5. Select the Source Field (e.g., "Front" where your Russian word is) and the Destination Field (e.g., "Audio").
6. Click **Generate**. It will download the `.mp3` files directly into your Anki database.

---

### Option 2: HyperTTS
HyperTTS is the newer, more powerful successor to AwesomeTTS. It was built to solve the clunkiness of managing API keys and batch processing. 
**Add-on Code:** `111623432`

#### Pros:
* Incredible batch processing (you can process thousands of cards at once smoothly).
* **Real-time preview:** You can hear exactly what the voice will sound like before generating the audio for your whole deck.
* Access to ultra-premium neural voices (Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, ElevenLabs) which understand Russian word stress beautifully. 
* Audio rule configurations (e.g., "if the text contains brackets, don't read the brackets aloud").

#### Cons:
* While there is a free tier (using basic voices), taking full advantage of the premium neural voices requires a "Language Tools" subscription (about $5/month) or configuring your own API keys.
* The interface has a steeper learning curve than AwesomeTTS.

#### How to use it:
1. Open the Anki Browser (click **Browse**).
2. Select your cards.
3. In the top menu, click **HyperTTS** -> **Add Audio**.
4. You will see a detailed configuration screen. 
5. Select your Target Field (where the audio will go) and your Source Field (the Russian text).
6. Choose a voice. If you configure a free trial of Language Tools within the plugin, choose a **Microsoft Azure Neural** voice (e.g., "Dmitry Neural" or "Svetlana Neural"). These are widely considered the best for Russian.
7. Click **Preview** on a few cards to ensure it sounds natural and respects stress marks.
8. Click **Apply to Notes** to batch generate and save the audio files.

### Which should you choose?
Try **AwesomeTTS** first if you just want to quickly generate some basic, free audio using Yandex or Google Translate to replace broken audio in your existing decks. 

Switch to **HyperTTS** when you are ready to build a high-quality deck from scratch or if you want to use advanced neural voices (like Azure) that perfectly handle the complex intonation of Russian sentences.
